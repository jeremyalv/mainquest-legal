MQ has no free tier: using the campaign requires an active weekly or annual auto-renewing subscription. There is no app-level free period. Payment is charged to your Apple/Google account when confirmed, except an explicitly offered eligible introductory free trial, whose charge starts when the trial ends.

Manage or cancel through your store subscription settings. Uninstalling MQ does not cancel a subscription. Store confirmation controls the transaction.

Access ends when the entitlement expires or is revoked. Offline access may continue until the last verified expiration; reconnect to refresh changes. A billing error does not erase local progress.

## Local data, permissions and availability

Progress is stored on your device. On iPhone it is also backed up automatically to your own iCloud (same Apple ID, no MQ account) and restored on reinstall; this is best effort and can take minutes. Without iCloud, reset, uninstall or device loss may remove it. Restoring a subscription restores access; progress comes back from iCloud when it is available. Notifications and telemetry are optional and can be controlled in the Hero tab or device settings. The privacy policy is in the app (Hero > Settings). Share destinations have their own terms and policies.
